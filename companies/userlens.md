## Public Description

First ever Agentic CSMs to upsell & renew 6+ figure deals.

Most B2B churn is preventable - most teams just see it too late. By the time a CSM notices, the champion has already quietly disengaged, key features went unadopted, and the renewal conversation is damage control. The dangerous part? The customer might still be logging in, but they've stopped using what actually matters. Userlens catches all of that months earlier. Our AI agents monitor every user at every account the way your best CSM watches your top five strategic accounts: - feature adoption - usage trends - champion engagement - sentiment shifts in Slack and Intercom - open support issues. When a signal hits (churn risk, renewal, expansion opportunity), Userlens doesn't just alert you. It builds the talking points and deck on top of your team's approved templates and playbook. If you have CSMs, Userlens makes every one of them your best. If you don't have one yet, Userlens runs your book like the best CSM money could buy.
