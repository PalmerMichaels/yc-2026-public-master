## Public Description

An AI-first CRM built for Startups

Fixture is an AI-native CRM built for startups. It integrates with all the tools you use to communicate with customers, tracks marketing channels (e.g. website traffic), and pulls everything into a single go-to-market context store. Our AI agents proactively help you move your deals forward, and you can use Fixture from our web UI or any of your favorite AI tools (e.g. Claude Code).
