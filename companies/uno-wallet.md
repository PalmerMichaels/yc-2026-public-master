## Public Description

The AI wallet that picks the best card at checkout.

Uno is building the AI wallet that replaces Apple Pay. At checkout, Uno automatically picks the best credit card for every purchase based on merchant category, rewards structure, and spending patterns. Users earn more points, miles, and cashback without thinking about it. That's the wedge. The vision is a financial agent that automates every money decision in your pocket. Founded by Saket Joshi (www.linkedin.com/in/saket-dattatray-joshi/) and Mordi Shadpour (www.linkedin.com/in/mordi-shadpour/)
