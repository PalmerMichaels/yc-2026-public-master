## Public Description

AI CMO for Software Companies

AI marketing service for developer tools. We turn your real product into social posts and demo videos in your voice automatically. Think of it as a clone of you that's also marketing your company at the same time. Memoir is one system. It reads your repo, drives your live staging app with an AI agent, generates branded demo videos with synthetic data so dashboards look real and narrates them in a voice cloned from yours. For the social side, we monitor what's actually getting traction in your space right now. The threads going viral on dev-tools X, the posts climbing on HN, the formats your audience is engaging with this week, and we draft posts in your writing voice that ride those currents. You review everything before it goes out. The output is indistinguishable from how you'd write and record it yourself, if you had the time and a full marketing team behind you.
