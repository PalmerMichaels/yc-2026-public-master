## Public Description

AI Bloomberg Terminal built for Healthcare (and beyond)

Soria is an AI-native Bloomberg Terminal for investors who specialize in a particular sector, starting with healthcare. Our agents convert hundreds of the most important public and private sources into an always-on financial model, reviewed by domain experts and delivered to hedge funds, banks, and asset managers through a terminal UI, MCP, API, warehouse sync, and research feed. Unlike our competitors, we own the data layer and expand bottom-up, one sector at a time - additionally our agents proactively do research on behalf of our clients without them lifting a finger or sharing any data.
