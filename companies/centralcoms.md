## Public Description

AI Agents for Property Management

CentralComs is building AI Agents for property managers that tracks every maintenance call, chases every open ticket, and keep owners/tenants/vendors updated, so nothing falls through the cracks and operations never stall. These agent work directly inside AppFolio, Buildium, Yardi and your existing stack, making real-time decisions and resolving multi-step workflows without handoffs.
