Open-source infrastructure for reliable long-running agents

YC recruited us into the current batch and we've since hired two senior founding team members (ex-Google, Forbes 30u30 harness engineer). All three founding team members are two time founders. We just closed a $1m capped SAFE from Jonathan Abrams 8-Bit Capital and expect to raise $2.5-$3m more on Demo Day.
