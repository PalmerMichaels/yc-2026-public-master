AI Native Sourcing Broker For Overseas Manufacturing

The factories that actually deliver sit behind decades-old relationships outsiders can't break into. We were born into them. Every deal runs through a real human broker, supercharged by AI that handles sourcing, quoting, QC, and logistics. 10x faster, at a fraction of the cost. Sourcing is the largest broken market in global trade. We're the on-ramp.
