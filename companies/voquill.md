## Public Description

The modern AI-native operating system for medical labs.

Voquill is the modern AI-native operating system for medical labs. This software manages the entire workflow of the lab (accessioning, workflow, reporting, coding, compliance, and revenue) and contains modern agentic AI tools to help each step. For example, pathologists can write reports 20% faster through our voice AI coworker. Incumbent lab operating systems are old, don't scale with how modern labs work, and can’t integrate existing AI solutions. As a result, labs are either building their own or paying millions for band-aid solutions and consultants. We're building an agentic platform to manage every aspect of the lab and displace the billions spent on legacy management systems.
