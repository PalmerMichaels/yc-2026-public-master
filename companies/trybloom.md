## Public Description

Brand infrastructure for AI

Bloom is the brand layer for agents - it turns brand into infrastructure that any agent, app, or platform can call. Ingest your brand from anywhere - decks, websites, social, Figma, and more. Bloom systemises your brand, enabling the creation of on-brand outputs like images, video, video, copy, and more, through the web app, the API, or MCP. We're building brand-as-infrastructure for the AI era.
