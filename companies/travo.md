## Public Description

The data infrastructure for real estate

We are building the most accurate and comprehensive real estate data set. Travo is the one stop shop for anyone working in real estate to search any property or parcel of land and instantly access rental comps, ownership, zoning and financials. With our data, we build the best informed AI applications for real estate private equity firms, developers, and brokers.
