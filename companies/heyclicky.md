## Public Description

An AI buddy that lives on your Mac.

HeyClicky sits right next to your cursor and sees everything you see. ask a question out loud and it walks you through whatever you're working on. Or say “HeyClicky Agent” and it'll spin up an agent to build, research, or do whatever for you in the background.
