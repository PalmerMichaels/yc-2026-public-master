## Public Description

Customize your software to every user with AI

Gigacatalyst allows sales and CS teams to build missing features that customers need, so you stop losing business because of feature gaps. We’ve helped our customers unblock $1M in sales in 6 weeks. We do this with an embedded AI customization layer that learns your APIs. Each customer can now adapt your product to match their workflows perfectly, and share it across their team with our in-built app store. The result is that customers use your SaaS more, retain for longer, and expand quicker, because they get bespoke implementation for their exact usecase, without waiting. We're live with the top Series B companies with 90% repeat usage rate. Our customers tell us that Gigacatalyst is the best way to increase usage, retention, and expansion in 2026.
