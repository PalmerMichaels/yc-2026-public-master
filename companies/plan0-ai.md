## Public Description

AI native construction cost estimation & analytics.

PLAN0 is building the Bloomberg of construction for real estate developers, investors, and general contractors — $20B of projects are already running through the platform. We analyze architectural plans using vision models to generate detailed cost estimates and analytics in minutes. Through large scale industry partnerships, the platform also aggregates historical and real-time project data across all major geographies to power ML-driven analytics, decomposing costs into market-observable factors and predicting local trade-level pricing up to 24 months out.
