## Public Description

AI-native operating system for long term care providers

TakeCareOS is building the infrastructure for the AI-native long term care providers. Today’s care providers are held back by massive administrative overhead, buried under fragmented tools and manual workflows. TakeCareOS consolidates the entire care lifecycle—scheduling, billing, documentation, compliance, customer support etc.—into a single, intelligent system driven by AI agents. We are eliminating the operational friction of care coordination to establish a new standard of high-efficiency, highly scalable care practices.
